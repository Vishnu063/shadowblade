Review notes

  • Replaced Kael's cylinder/box body with a Blender-authored tapered figure, modeled face, swept hair, layered vest/linen, belt/pouch, laced boots, bracers and cloth scarf. Added breathing, knee bend and grounded/jump poses while retaining combat timings.

  • Replaced triangle-strip rock walls with textured, irregular, instanced stone geometry and varied overlap. The original collision-aligned walking surface remains simple and reliable.

Gaps that remain

The concept still has much richer vegetation, more organic cliff composition, superior material painting, cloud/atmosphere detail, character animation and cinematic lighting. The current stones and waterfall framing remain visibly modular; the distant castle is legacy art. NPC duplication outside this scene has not been solved in this milestone.
